# Brand Context — Houston TX Hot Chicken (HHC), UK launch

Reference for copy, tone and proof points when building the HHC UK pre-launch page.
Visual/brand tokens live in `/CLAUDE.md`. Source: official UK-debut press release (22 June 2026)
and hhc.ooo/uk.

## In one line
Houston TX Hot Chicken (HHC) is a cult Las Vegas-based fast-casual Nashville-style hot chicken
brand making its first international move with a UK debut.

## The story / positioning
- One of the fastest-growing hot chicken concepts in the US; founded 2021 by Edmond Barseghian.
- Enters the UK via a development deal with PizzaExpress: 3 locations opening in 2026, 50 planned
  over the next three years. UK opening locations and dates announced in the coming weeks.
- Brand built on an unapologetically bold menu plus an electric experience that blends craveable
  food with car culture and social-media-ready energy.

## Product
- Signature: the Original Hot Chicken Sandwich, across a range of heat levels.
- The top heat is "Houston, We Have a Problem!" — a waiver-required ~2 million Scoville experience
  that goes viral at every market entry.
- Full menu: hot chicken sandwiches, tenders and waffles, nuggets, loaded fries, house-made
  lemonades, and HHC's famous house sauce.

## Proof points (use selectively; one cue per section, do not stack)
- Nearly 400,000 social media followers nationwide.
- FastCasual 2026 Top 100 Movers & Shakers; No. 217 on the Inc. 5000.
- Hot Concepts Award (Nation's Restaurant News); Breakout Brand on the Franchise Times Top 400.
- Backed by Savory Fund (Utah private equity, $750M+ AUM, 18-year restaurant-scaling track record).
- US footprint: Arizona, California, Nevada, Washington, Texas, Utah, Idaho, Michigan, Ohio.

## Partnership voices (quotes available if a press/quote block is needed)
- Brian Simowitz, President, Houston TX Hot Chicken — on the PizzaExpress tie-up and bringing
  "something unexpected to UK consumers".
- Andrew K. Smith, Managing Director, Savory Fund — "genuine cultural energy".
- Paula MacKenzie, Group CEO, PizzaExpress — "bold flavor, real energy, an experience that stands apart".

## Voice and tone
- Confident, bold, playful, high-energy. Heat, car culture, viral/social-ready.
- British English for UK copy. No em dashes (per CLAUDE.md).
- Note tension: the US brand voice is American ("flavor", "color"); UK page copy must be British
  English. Keep the energy, localise the spelling.

## Pre-launch page angle
- Single job: capture emails for the UK launch (locations/dates land "in the coming weeks", so the
  waitlist promise is genuine: be first to know where and when).
- Anticipation cues that fit the brand: the heat-level ladder up to the 2M Scoville waiver challenge,
  "now landing in the UK", PizzaExpress partnership credibility.

## Source: full press release (verbatim)

**Houston TX Hot Chicken takes its heat international with UK debut**

The cult Nashville hot chicken brand enters the UK through a development deal with PizzaExpress, with
three locations opening in 2026 and 50 planned over the next three years.

Las Vegas, NV / Draper, UT / London, UK — June 22, 2026 — Houston TX Hot Chicken (HHC), the
Las Vegas-based fast-casual brand that has become one of the fastest-growing hot chicken concepts in
the United States, today announced its first international expansion with a debut in the United Kingdom.

"Working with PizzaExpress to expand internationally is exciting. No one knows fried chicken better
than HHC, and no one knows the UK better than PizzaExpress. We're ready to bring something unexpected
to UK consumers." — Brian Simowitz, President of Houston TX Hot Chicken

"HHC has built something rare with genuine cultural energy. Taking that to the UK through
PizzaExpress—with their scale and market knowledge—accelerates the brand's trajectory. This is a
significant moment." — Andrew K. Smith, Managing Director of Savory Fund

"We're thrilled to add Houston Hot Chicken to our brands. It brings bold flavor, real energy, and an
experience that stands apart." — Paula MacKenzie, Group CEO of PizzaExpress

The partnership pairs HHC's cult-favorite brand and proven fast-casual model with PizzaExpress's
unmatched operational infrastructure and deep understanding of the UK consumer — a combination the two
companies are betting will shake up Britain's QSR landscape.

HHC has built its US following on the strength of an unapologetically bold menu and an electric brand
experience that blends craveable food with car culture and social-media-ready energy. The brand's
signature offering — the Original Hot Chicken Sandwich — is available across a range of heat levels,
culminating in "Houston, We Have a Problem!," a waiver-required 2 million Scoville unit experience that
has become a viral phenomenon at every market entry. The full menu includes hot chicken sandwiches,
tenders and waffles, nuggets, loaded fries, house-made lemonades, and HHC's famous house sauce.

The UK expansion marks a major milestone for HHC following a period of explosive domestic growth.
Founded in 2021 by Edmond Barseghian, the brand has scaled to locations across Arizona, California,
Nevada, Washington, Texas, Utah, Idaho, Michigan, and Ohio, and has cultivated a fanbase of nearly
400,000 social media followers nationwide. The brand was recently ranked on FastCasual's 2026 Top 100
Movers & Shakers and has earned industry recognition including a No. 217 ranking on the Inc. 5000, the
Hot Concepts Award from Nation's Restaurant News, and Breakout Brand honors on the Franchise Times Top 400.

HHC's US growth is backed by Savory Fund, a Utah-based private equity firm with over $750 million in
assets under management and an 18-year track record of scaling high-potential restaurant brands.

Further details on UK opening locations and dates will be announced in the coming weeks.

For more information, visit www.hhc.ooo.
