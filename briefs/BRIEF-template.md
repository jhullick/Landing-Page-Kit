# Landing Page Brief (fill in per page, then paste into Claude Code)

Build a coming-soon / pre-launch landing page whose single job is to capture email addresses.

SUBJECT: [Brand name] is [what it is in one line].
AUDIENCE: [who, where; note: UK].
SINGLE GOAL: collect emails for launch updates. No other CTAs.
VALUE PROPOSITION (one sentence): For [audience], [brand] is the [category] that [differentiator]
  because [reason to believe].
WAITLIST PROMISE (why give their email now): [early access / founder pricing / exclusive content].
TONE / FEEL: [e.g. confident, warm, premium; "feels like [reference], not a generic template"].
VISUAL REFERENCE: [screenshot or link, if any].
LAUNCH WINDOW: [e.g. "Autumn 2026"].

PAGE STRUCTURE (top to bottom):
1. Hero: benefit-led headline naming one outcome; one-line subhead (what it is + who for);
   email-only signup form; one proof or anticipation cue.
2. Optional: 3 short benefit points OR one product screenshot/illustration.
3. Optional: brief "what to expect / how it works".
4. Repeat email CTA before the footer.
5. Footer: brand identity, privacy policy link, social links.

FORM:
- Single email field, top-aligned label "Email address", action button
  "[Join the waitlist / Get early access / Notify me]".
- Consent line under the form (the uk-consent skill provides the wording).
- Helper microcopy: "No spam. Unsubscribe at any time."

CONSTRAINTS: mobile and desktop responsive; [light/dark] theme; accessible per CLAUDE.md.

Apply the landing-page-quality, coming-soon-pattern and uk-consent skills.
