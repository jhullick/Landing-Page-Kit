# Landing Page Brief: Houston TX Hot Chicken (HHC) UK coming-soon

Build a coming-soon / pre-launch landing page whose single job is to capture email addresses.

SUBJECT: Houston TX Hot Chicken (HHC) is America's cult Nashville-style hot chicken brand, now
  bringing its heat to the UK.
AUDIENCE: UK fried-chicken fans and heat-seekers; social-savvy food lovers, skewing 18-34, urban
  (London and major cities first). UK.
SINGLE GOAL: collect emails for launch updates. No other CTAs.
VALUE PROPOSITION (one sentence): For UK heat-seekers, HHC is the cult American hot chicken brand
  that serves Nashville-style heat across every level, because it has built a 400,000-strong US
  following on bold flavour and viral energy and is now landing in Britain with PizzaExpress.
WAITLIST PROMISE (why give their email now): be first to know where and when HHC opens in the UK.
  Locations and opening dates go to the list first.
TONE / FEEL: confident, bold, high-energy and playful; car-culture and social-ready swagger. Feels
  like a hyped US street brand arriving in town, not a generic QSR template.
VISUAL REFERENCE: hhc.ooo/uk. Dark theme, Bitter display with Lato body, red #e30513 heat accent.
LAUNCH WINDOW: first UK locations in 2026 (exact sites and dates announced soon).

PAGE STRUCTURE (top to bottom):
1. Hero: arrival-led headline naming the outcome ("America's cult hot chicken is coming to the UK");
   one-line subhead (what it is + who it is for); email-only signup form; one anticipation cue
   (e.g. "Opening in the UK, 2026" or "Nearly 400,000 fans in the US"). Pick one cue, do not stack.
2. The signature element: the heat ladder running up to the waiver-required 2 million Scoville
   "Houston, We Have a Problem!" challenge. (Alternative: three short anticipation points.)
3. Brief "what to expect": the bold menu (Original Hot Chicken Sandwich across heat levels, tenders
   and waffles, loaded fries, house sauce), with the PizzaExpress partnership as a credibility cue.
4. Repeat email CTA before the footer.
5. Footer: HHC brand identity, privacy policy link, social links.

FORM:
- Single email field, top-aligned label "Email address", action button "Sign me up".
- Consent line under the form (the uk-consent skill provides the wording: names HHC and the email
  channel, unticked opt-in, link to the privacy notice).
- Helper microcopy: "No spam. Unsubscribe at any time."

CONSTRAINTS: mobile and desktop responsive; dark theme; accessible per CLAUDE.md.
