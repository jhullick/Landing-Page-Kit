# UK Email Consent (PECR + UK GDPR)

General information, not legal advice. Have the final wording checked by a qualified person.

## Lawful basis
- For a new B2C brand collecting emails before launch, the lawful route is CONSENT: freely given,
  specific, informed and unambiguous, via an unticked opt-in or a clear affirmative action.
- The "soft opt-in" exemption does NOT apply to a pre-launch waitlist, because there is no prior sale.
- A single, well-worded consent event satisfies both PECR (sending) and UK GDPR (processing).
  You do not need two checkboxes, but the wording must cover email marketing.

## On-page requirements
- An unticked opt-in (or unambiguous affirmative action) that names the brand and the channel (email).
- A link to a privacy notice: who you are, what you will do with the address, the right to withdraw.
- Clear sender identity. Every later marketing email must carry a working unsubscribe.
- Do not pre-tick. Do not bundle consent into other terms. Ask separately for SMS if used.
- Double opt-in is not strictly required but is strong evidence of valid consent; sensible for a new list.

## Example wording (adapt, then get legal review)
"Yes, I'd like [Brand] to email me news and launch updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
See our Privacy Policy."
